# Play it again

Two dots next to a thick double line mean **repeat**: go back and play that part once more. The repeat starts at the sign that faces the other way, or at the beginning of the song.

::staff music="|: G4/q G4 G4 A4 | B4/h A4 :|"

::quiz "What do you do at a repeat sign?" options="Go back and play that part again* | Stop | Play faster"
