# Si♭: a flat

A **flat** (♭) is the opposite of a sharp: it makes a note a half step **lower**. Si♭ sits between La and Si, on the middle line with a ♭ in front.

## Si♭

Si♭ is a **forked** fingering, like Fa: thumb and hole 1, **skip hole 2**, then cover holes 3 and 4.

::fingering note=Bb4

::try "Play Si♭" note=Bb4 hold=1

## The key of F

Songs in the **key of F** have one flat in the key signature, on the Si line: every Si is a Si♭.
