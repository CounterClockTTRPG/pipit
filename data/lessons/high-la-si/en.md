# Even higher: La' and Si'

## La'

High La is written on a short extra line **above the staff**, a **ledger line**. It is low La with a pinched thumb: holes 1 and 2.

::fingering note=A5

::try "Play high La" note=A5 hold=1

## Si'

High Si sits just **above** that ledger line. Pinched thumb, holes 1 and 2, and holes 4 and 5. It feels odd at first: hole 3 stays open in the middle.

::fingering note=B5

::try "Play high Si" note=B5 hold=1
