# Pinch the thumb

The high notes use a trick: you **pinch** the thumb hole. Bend your left thumb so that the tip of your **nail** rests in the hole and leaves a tiny gap at the top. That little gap lets the recorder jump up an octave.

::pip "Pinching takes practice. If you hear the low note, open the gap a tiny bit more; if it squeaks, close it a little."

## Mi'

High Mi is in the **top space** of the staff. Finger low Mi (holes 1 to 5), then pinch the thumb. Blow a bit faster than for low Mi.

::fingering note=E5

::try "Play high Mi" note=E5 hold=1

## Fa'

High Fa sits on the **top line**. Pinched thumb, holes 1 to 4, and hole 6; holes 5 and 7 stay open.

::fingering note=F5

::try "Play high Fa" note=F5 hold=1

## Sol'

High Sol, in the space **above the staff**, is low Sol with a pinched thumb: holes 1, 2 and 3.

::fingering note=G5

::try "Play high Sol" note=G5 hold=1
