# Rests

A **rest** is a beat of silence. You stop blowing, but keep counting. Rests have their own shapes:

::staff music="B4/q R/q B4/q R/q | A4/h R/h"

- A **quarter rest** (a little zigzag) lasts 1 beat.
- A **half rest** (a small block sitting on the middle line) lasts 2 beats.

A rest is a good moment to breathe.
