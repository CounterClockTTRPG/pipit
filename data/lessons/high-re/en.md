# A high note: Re'

High Re is written on the **fourth line** of the staff. It looks easy but feels strange: take your **thumb off** the back hole, and cover only the second hole with your left middle finger.

::fingering note=D5

Blow a little faster than for Sol, but not harder. If it squeaks, blow more gently.

::try "Play high Re" note=D5 hold=1

::exercise "From Si to Re' and back" music="B4/q D5 D5/h | B4/q A4 G4/h" tempo=66

::pip "With Re' you can play Mary Had a Little Lamb."
