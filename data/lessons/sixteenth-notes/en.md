# Sixteenth notes

A **sixteenth note** lasts a quarter of a beat: four of them fit in one beat. They are joined by **two** beams instead of one.

::staff music="G4/s A4 B4 A4 G4/q G4/s A4 B4 A4 G4/q | B4/s C5 D5 C5 B4/q A4/h"

Count "one-e-and-a": one note on each syllable. Tongue lightly and keep your fingers close to the holes, so they don't have far to travel.

::play "Listen first" music="G4/s A4 B4 A4 G4/q G4/s A4 B4 A4 G4/q | B4/s C5 D5 C5 B4/q A4/h" tempo=60
