# Three beats in a bar

So far every bar had **four** beats. The two numbers at the start of a song, the **time signature**, tell you how many: the top number is the beats in a bar. In **3/4** there are three, and you count "**one**, two, three, **one**, two, three". The first beat is the strongest.

::staff music="G4/q A4 B4 | C5/h B4/q | A4/q B4 A4 | G4/h." time="3/4"

The last note is a **dotted half note**: a half note plus half again, so it fills the whole bar of three beats.

::quiz "How many beats does a dotted half note last?" options="3* | 2 | 4"
