# Flats up high, and Do''

Baroque music often has more than one flat. These are the last notes you need for the minuets and sonatas.

## Mi♭'

High Mi♭, in the top space with a ♭: **thumb off**, cover holes 2 to 6. Hole 1 and hole 7 stay open.

::fingering note=Eb5

::try "Play high Mi♭" note=Eb5 hold=1

## Si♭'

High Si♭, above the ledger line: pinched thumb, holes 1 and 2, then holes 4, 5 and 6.

::fingering note=Bb5

::try "Play high Si♭" note=Bb5 hold=1

## La♭'

High La♭ is the same note as **Sol♯'**: one key, two names. Pinched thumb, holes 1, 2 and 4.

::fingering note=Ab5

## Do''

Very high Do sits on the **second ledger line** above the staff: pinched thumb, hole 1, and holes 4 and 5. Blow fast but gently, and pinch a little tighter.

::fingering note=C6

::try "Play very high Do" note=C6 hold=1

## Re''

Very high Re, just above Do'': pinched thumb, holes 1, 3 and 4, and holes 6 and 7. It is the top note of most recorders; you need it for Telemann's Andante.

::fingering note=D6

::try "Play very high Re" note=D6 hold=1
