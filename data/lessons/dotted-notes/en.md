# The dot

A **dot** after a note makes it half as long again. A dotted quarter note lasts **one and a half beats**, and is often followed by an eighth note that fills up the beat.

::staff music="B4/q. A4/e A4/h | A4/q. G4/e G4/h"

Count "one, two-and": the long note covers "one, two", the short one sits on "and".

::play "Listen first" music="B4/q. A4/e A4/h | A4/q. G4/e G4/h" tempo=72
