# How long is a note?

The shape of a note tells you how many beats it lasts:

- A **quarter note** (black, with a stem) lasts **1 beat**.
- A **half note** (white, with a stem) lasts **2 beats**.
- A **whole note** (white, no stem) lasts **4 beats**.

::staff music="B4/q B4 B4 B4 | B4/h B4 | B4/w"

Count while you play: "one, two, three, four". Keep blowing for the whole length of a long note, until the next one starts.
