# Six-eight time

In **6/8** a bar holds six eighth notes, in **two groups of three**. You feel two big beats, each split in three: "**one**-two-three, **four**-five-six". It sounds like rocking or skipping.

::staff music="G4/e A4 B4 C5 B4 A4 | G4/q A4/e B4/q. | B4/q A4/e G4/q A4/e | G4/q. G4/q." time="6/8"

A **dotted quarter note** fills one group of three, and a quarter plus an eighth makes the skipping "long-short" rhythm.

::quiz "How do you feel a bar of 6/8?" options="Two groups of three* | Six equal beats in a row | Three groups of two"
