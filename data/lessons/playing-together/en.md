# Playing together

Music is more fun with others. Many songs in Pipit have a **band**: a bass line, a second recorder or a harpsichord that plays along while you play the tune. Switch on **Band** above the song to hear it.

::pip "Use headphones for the band. Otherwise the microphone hears the band as well as you, and I can't tell who is playing."

When you play together, **nobody waits**. The band keeps going at its own speed, so keep counting, even after a wrong note: just join in again at the next note.

::quiz "You play a wrong note while the band plays along. What do you do?" options="Keep going and join in at the next note* | Stop and start again | Play faster to catch up"

## A round

In a **round**, everybody plays the same tune, but each player starts a little later. *Frère Jacques* is a famous round: when you reach the second line, the band starts the first line again, and the two fit together.
