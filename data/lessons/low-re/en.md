# Going low: Re

Low Re sits just **below the staff**. Cover the back hole and the first six holes, leaving only the bottom double hole open. Your right hand now joins in: index, middle and ring finger on holes 4, 5 and 6.

::fingering note=D4

Low notes need **very gentle, slow air**. If it squeaks or jumps up, blow even softer and check that every hole is completely covered.

::try "Play low Re" note=D4 hold=1.5

::exercise "From Sol down to Re" music="G4/q D4 G4/h | G4/q A4 B4/h | A4/q G4 D4/h | G4/w" tempo=66

::pip "Most beginners blow low notes too hard. Gentle air is the secret."
