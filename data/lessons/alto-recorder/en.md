# The alto recorder

The **alto** recorder is the soprano's bigger sister. Its lowest note, with every hole closed, is **Fa** instead of Do, so it sounds lower and warmer. In the time of Bach, Handel and Telemann the alto was *the* recorder: their sonatas were written for it.

::pip "On a soprano, I move alto music up or down so it fits. You'll see a ↕ on the song."

## Same fingers, other names

The fingerings on an alto are the **same patterns** as on the soprano, but every pattern plays a different note. All holes closed is Fa, not Do; thumb and holes 1 to 3 is Do', not Sol. So the fingers stay the same and the names move.

::quiz "On an alto recorder, which note do you get with every hole closed?" options="Fa* | Do | Sol"

## Adding your alto

If you have an alto, add it in **Settings** under recorders and pick it at the top of a song. Pipit then shows alto fingerings, and the baroque sonatas play at their real pitch.
