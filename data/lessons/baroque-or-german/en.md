# Baroque or German?

Recorders come with two different **fingering systems**. They differ in only a few notes, and Fa is the one you meet first.

- **Baroque** fingering is the original one, from the time of Bach and Handel. Fa uses a *forked* fingering, with a gap in the middle.
- **German** fingering was invented around 1926 by Peter Harlan to make Fa simpler for beginners: you just lift the fingers from the bottom up.

::fingering "Baroque: forked" note=F4 system=baroque

::fingering "German: simple" note=F4 system=german

The catch: on a German recorder some other notes, like Fa♯, need *harder* fingerings, and it plays less well in tune in other keys. That is why most teachers and books today use baroque recorders.

## Which one is yours?

Your recorder can tell you. Play Fa both ways: the fingering that sounds better in tune is the one your recorder was built for.

::compare "Play Fa both ways" note=F4
