# Fa♯: a sharp

A **sharp** (♯) in front of a note makes it a little higher: a **half step**, the smallest step on the recorder. Fa♯ sits between Fa and Sol.

## Fa♯

Thumb, holes 1, 2 and 3, then **skip hole 4** and cover holes 5 and 6. Hole 7 stays open.

::fingering note=F#4

::try "Play Fa♯" note=F#4 hold=1

## Fa♯'

High Fa♯, on the top line, works the same way: **pinched** thumb, holes 1, 2 and 3, and hole 5.

::fingering note=F#5

::try "Play high Fa♯" note=F#5 hold=1

## The key signature

When a song uses Fa♯ all the time, the sharp is written **once**, at the start of every line, on the Fa line. This is the **key signature**. It means: every Fa is a Fa♯, high or low, unless the music says otherwise. Songs in the **key of G** have one sharp.
