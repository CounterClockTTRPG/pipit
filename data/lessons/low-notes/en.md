# The bottom of the recorder

## Mi

Mi sits on the **bottom line**. Cover the back hole and holes 1 to 5; the two bottom holes stay open, so your right **ring finger** and little finger are up.

::fingering note=E4

::try "Play Mi" note=E4 hold=1

## Fa

Fa, in the **first space**, uses a *forked* fingering on most recorders: from Mi, keep your right **index** finger down, lift the **middle** finger, and put the **ring** and **little** fingers down. The gap in the middle is the "fork".

::fingering note=F4

::try "Play Fa" note=F4 hold=1

::pip "Some school recorders use \"German\" fingering, with a simpler Fa. Check Settings if your Fa sounds wrong."

## Do

Low Do, on a short line below the staff, is the lowest note of all: **every hole closed**, including both small double holes. Blow as gently as you can.

::fingering note=C4

::try "Play low Do" note=C4 hold=1.5

::exercise "Climb up from the bottom" music="C4/q D4 E4 F4 | G4/w | G4/q F4 E4 D4 | C4/w" tempo=60
