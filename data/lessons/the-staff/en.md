# The staff

Music is written on five lines, called the **staff**. Every line and every space between the lines is a note. The higher a note is written, the higher it sounds.

At the start is the **treble clef**. On a soprano recorder it has a little 8 on top, because the recorder sounds an octave higher than the notes look.

::staff music="B4/w | A4/w"

- **Si** sits on the **middle line**.
- **La** sits in the **space just below** it.

::quiz "Which note sits on the middle line of the staff?" options="Si* | La | Do"

::quiz "La is written..." options="in the space just below the middle line* | on the top line | below the staff"

Now read and play. Follow the cursor: it shows which note to play.
