# A second note: La

For La, keep everything from Si and add your left **middle finger** on the second hole.

::fingering note=A4

::try "Play La" note=A4 hold=1

Now switch: Si, La, Si, La. Only one finger moves.

::quiz "Which finger goes down to change Si into La?" options="Left middle finger* | Left thumb | Right index finger"

## Playing on the beat

Music has a steady **beat**, like a clock ticking. Pipit gives you four clicks to count you in: "one, two, three, four". Then play one note on every click.

::exercise "Play Si on every click, then La on every click" music="B4/q B4 B4 B4 | A4/q A4 A4 A4" tempo=60

::pip "Tongue every note with \"du\", so each one starts clearly."
