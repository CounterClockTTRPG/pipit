# Eighth notes

An **eighth note** lasts half a beat, so two of them fit in one beat. They often come in pairs or fours, joined by a thick line called a **beam**.

::staff music="G4/e G4 G4 G4 A4 A4 A4 A4 | B4/q A4 G4/h"

Count "one-and, two-and": one note on the number, one on the "and". Tongue each one lightly: "du-du, du-du".
