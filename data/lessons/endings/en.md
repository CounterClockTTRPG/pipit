# Two different endings

Sometimes a repeated part ends differently the second time. A bracket marked **1.** is played the first time only; the second time you skip it and play the bracket marked **2.** instead.

::staff music="|: B4/q B4 B4/h | [1 A4/q A4 A4/h :| [2 G4/w"

So you play: bar 1, ending 1, bar 1 again, ending 2.

::quiz "The second time through, which ending do you play?" options="The one marked 2.* | The one marked 1. | Both"
