# More sharps

Any note can get a sharp. A sharp written **in the music**, not in the key signature, is an **accidental**: it counts until the end of that bar.

## Do♯'

High Do♯ is between Do' and Re'. Take the **thumb off** and cover holes 1 and 2.

::fingering note=C#5

::try "Play Do♯'" note=C#5 hold=1

## Sol♯

Sol♯ is a tricky one: thumb, holes 1 and 2, holes 4 and 5, and **half** of hole 6 (only the larger of the two small holes). Hole 3 stays open.

::fingering note=G#4

::try "Play Sol♯" note=G#4 hold=1

## The natural sign

A **natural sign** (♮) cancels a sharp or a flat: the note goes back to normal. In the key of G, a Fa with a ♮ is a plain Fa again.

::staff music="A4/q G#4 A4 B4 | C5/q C#5 D5/h | D5/q C#5 C5 B4 | A4/q G#4 A4/h"

::quiz "A sharp appears in the middle of a bar. How long does it count?" options="Until the end of the bar* | Only for that one note | For the rest of the song"
